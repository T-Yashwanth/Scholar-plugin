# APA 7 Checklist (verifier)

The short form of `apa7-rules.md`, for checking drafts. If a source type is
not listed under "Reference formats" below, look up its pattern in
`apa7-rules.md` with Grep (search for the type name, for example `Podcast
episode` or `Statute`) instead of reading the whole file.

`*text*` means italic. Page and date ranges use a hyphen (dash rule).

## In-text citations

- Every citation has a reference entry and every entry is cited. Exceptions:
  personal communications and "as cited in" originals appear in text only.
- Author and year match the reference entry exactly.
- One author (Ahmed, 2016). Two authors every time (Wegener & Petty, 1994);
  narrative uses "and". Three or more: (Kernis et al., 1993) from the first
  citation.
- `&` only inside parentheses; `and` in running text.
- Group author: abbreviation defined at first use only if used again:
  (Mothers Against Drunk Driving [MADD], 2000), then (MADD, 2000).
- No author: title in the author slot; quotation marks for articles and
  pages, italics for standalone works. No date: (n.d.).
- Same author, same year: 2020a, 2020b, matching the reference list.
- Several sources in one parenthesis: alphabetical, semicolons.
- Direct quote: page (p. 12, pp. 12-13), paragraph (para. 4), section name,
  or timestamp. Required every time.
- Under 40 words: quotation marks, citation before the final period.
- 40 words or more: indented block, no quotation marks, citation after the
  final period.
- Secondary source: (as cited in Smith, 2003); only Smith is in the list.
- Unrecorded lecture or email: (A. Name, personal communication, Month Day,
  Year), not in the list.

## Reference list

- New page, `References` centered and bold, double-spaced, hanging indent.
- Alphabetical by first author's last name; same author: single-author
  entries first, oldest to newest.
- Authors: `Last, F. M.`, commas between, `&` before the last. List up to 20;
  21 or more: first 19, ellipsis, final author, no `&`.
- Date: `(Year).` for journals and books; `(Year, Month Day).` for news,
  webpages, videos, social media; `(n.d.).` when none.
- Article, chapter, and page titles inside a larger work: sentence case,
  plain.
- Standalone works (book, report, webpage, video): sentence case, italic.
- Journal name: title case, italic. Volume italic; issue in plain
  parentheses: `*Journal Name, 12*(3), 45-60.`
- Bracketed descriptions are plain: `[Video]`, `[PowerPoint slides]`.
- DOI as `https://doi.org/...` whenever one exists; no "DOI:" label.
- No URL for a library-database article without a DOI.
- No publisher location. No period after a DOI or URL.
- "Retrieved Month Day, Year, from" only for content that changes.

## Reference formats

- Journal: Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name, 12*(3), 45-60. https://doi.org/xx
- Book: Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book: Subtitle* (2nd ed.). Publisher.
- Chapter: Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), *Title of book* (pp. 10-25). Publisher.
- Report: Organization. (Year). *Title of report*. URL
- Webpage: Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL (drop Site Name when it equals the author)
- Newspaper online: Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title of article. *Newspaper*. URL
- Video: Uploader. (Year, Month Day). *Title* [Video]. YouTube. URL
- Course slides: Instructor, A. A. (Year). *Title* [PowerPoint slides]. Course Site Name. login URL
- Course lecture or transcript: Instructor, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title* [Video] or [Lecture transcript]. Course Site Name. login URL
- A published reading posted in the course site: cite the original work.

## Paper format (essays)

- Student title page: bold title, blank line, name, school, course,
  instructor, due date; centered in the upper half; page number top right;
  no running head.
- Title repeated, bold and centered, at the top of the first body page. No
  "Introduction" heading.
- Headings: 1 centered bold; 2 left bold; 3 left bold italic; 4 indented
  bold ending with a period, run in; 5 same as 4 plus italic.

## Punctuation

No em dash (U+2014) or en dash (U+2013) anywhere, including references.
